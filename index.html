Clinical Record • The Golden Standard
01

5 Mandatory Fields

Core Elements: 1. Clinical indication; 2. Offer timing; 3. Patient decision; 4. Chaperone full name & role; 5. Procedural outcome & concerns.

Contemporaneous Standard: Record every detail during or immediately after the consultation to ensure full legal defensibility.

02

SNOMED CT & Free Text

Software Limitation: SNOMED codes (Agreed: 1104081000000107, Refused: 763380007) only log the outcome, not the offer itself.

Mandatory Action: Clinicians must type free text explicitly stating the offer was made to provide verifiable legal evidence.

03

Refusals & Safety Limits

Patient Refusal: State clearly that the chaperone offer was made and declined by the patient under their informed autonomy.

Clinician Boundaries: If declining unaccompanied practice for safety reasons, document the offered alternatives (rescheduling or same-sex clinician).

Understanding SNOMED CT

The universal healthcare terminology system

SNOMED CT is the universal digital dictionary that translates everyday clinical language into a standardised code the computer can understand.

The Core Concept: Human Words in Front, Digital Barcodes Behind

The Clinician's Role

Clinicians speak, type, and read standard medical English (e.g. searching terms like "heart attack", "MI", or "myocardial infarction"). They never have to memorise or manually look up numeric codes.

The Computer's Role

The Electronic Health Record (EHR) matches the chosen term to a unique numerical identifier (e.g. 22298006), automatically attaching this digital "barcode" to the patient's record in the background.

Why It Matters

  • ✓ Captures Synonyms: Whether a GP types "high blood pressure" or a hospital doctor types "essential hypertension", the system recognises both mean the exact same clinical entity.
  • ✓ Eliminates Ambiguity: A single code ensures that records transferred between GP surgeries, hospitals, and community clinics retain their precise meaning without mistranslation.
  • ✓ Powers Clinical Safety: Because the system understands the exact medical concept, it can automatically trigger safety alerts (such as allergy warnings or drug contraindications) and support audit reporting.
